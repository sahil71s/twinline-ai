# 3–5 minute demo script

1. **Overview:** “This is the line twin. Notice the three sensor tiers.”
2. **Drift:** choose hidden_degradation; show ST-12 amber risk and evidence.
3. **Confidence:** explain why direct sensors produce higher confidence than inferred stations.
4. **Late defect:** open Defect Genealogy; select a failed VIN; trace backward to the likely source and forward to affected vehicles.
5. **Containment:** open What-If; compare targeted quarantine with full-line stop.
6. **Human decision:** approve/override and show the log.
7. **Validation:** show precision/recall/FPR/lead time and explicitly label synthetic results.
8. **Close:** “We do not automate the factory decision; we make the decision safer, earlier and more targeted.”
