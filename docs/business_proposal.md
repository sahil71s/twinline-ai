# Business Proposal — TwinLine.ai v2

## Evolution from Round 1

Round 1 established the problem of invisible ripple effects and the Sense → Understand → Predict → Confidence → Simulate → Recommend → Act flow. Round 2 turns that concept into a working mechanism.

The hardest manufacturing question is not simply “did a defect happen?” It is: **when a defect is discovered late, where did it likely originate, which vehicles were touched, which are still in the plant, and what is the least disruptive safe action?**

TwinLine.ai answers that with a vehicle-level digital twin, confidence-aware analytics, defect genealogy and what-if simulation.

## Differentiation

1. **Defect Genealogy Tracer** — vehicle-level lineage rather than station-only alerts.
2. **Real confidence** — sensor coverage and evidence affect confidence; the system can abstain.
3. **Human-in-the-loop** — recommendations are simulated and logged; no autonomous PLC control.

## Business case

Illustrative assumptions only: if targeted containment saves 17 minutes versus a 20-minute full-line stop, and two incidents/week are caught, then avoided stoppage is `17 × 2 × assumed cost/minute`. The prototype defaults to $22K/min because that figure appeared in the Round 1 strategy; this is **not a client fact** and must be replaced by validated plant economics.
